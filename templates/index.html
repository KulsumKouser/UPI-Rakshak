STAY ALERT

Important UPI Safety Tips

  • Never share your UPI PIN or OTP with anyone.
  • Never enter your PIN to receive money.
  • Avoid clicking unknown payment links.
  • Verify payment requests before approving them.
  • Contact your bank through its official channels if you suspect fraud.
